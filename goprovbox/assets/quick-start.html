Circuit Tools 3 compatibility is still in beta. Circuit Tools can reject generated VBO files with an authenticity/checksum error and may close. This remains unresolved. Re-encoding is not a proven fix. A successful export verifies the app's output, not acceptance by Circuit Tools. Test a short run before processing a full day.

Install once. Work locally.

  1. Download the Windows Setup.exe from the official releases page. You do not need Python or a GitHub account.
  2. Run the installer. It installs for your Windows account and adds a Start menu shortcut. A desktop shortcut is optional. Choose Help → Uninstall app to open the normal Windows uninstall confirmation. The app closes so its files can be removed. Recordings, exports, settings and downloaded video tools are retained. For a portable copy, close the app and delete its extracted app folder; keep your recordings and exports separately.
  3. Open GoPro VBOX Sync. If video tools are missing, the app offers a 110 MB download of FFmpeg and FFprobe from Gyan's GitHub release, with an integrity check. Allow about 500 MB free space for setup. If either tool is missing when you scan or try the demo, the app offers this download and resumes after setup. You can also choose Help → Set up video tools. Internet access is needed for the download; processing then works offline.

Windows 10/11, 64-bit Intel/AMD. This beta installer is unsigned, so Windows may display an unknown-publisher warning. Verify the official download and its published SHA-256 before deciding whether to install. ARM Windows is not tested.

1Choose a folder

Put the GoPro MP4 files in the folder with your VBOX runs. Click Browse, then Scan.

The original VBOX MP4 videos can stay in the folder too; the app identifies the GoPro files automatically. Subfolders are ignored. Keep the camera originals; edited movies may lose GPS metadata. GPS must have been enabled and recorded a usable fix.

2Select your runs

All matched videos start ticked. Untick the Include column to skip any run. Click the video row to see its preview and adjust rotation if needed. Rotation starts at Upright — no rotation. Use Frame & preview… to adjust the selected video’s crop.

The previewed row and included videos are separate choices. Space toggles the focused row. If nothing is ticked, Create files is disabled.

3Preview and create

Choose resolution and overlay, then click Frame & preview…. Only export video with VBOX data is ticked by default: it removes footage before and after matching periods. Untick it to keep the full videos. Check that the image is upright, all gauges fit and their units are correct. Choose Save to, then Create files.

Open Report for checks and warnings. Keep each generated MP4 beside its VBO when moving or sharing the result. Open the VBO in Circuit Tools 3 for analysis; the MP4 also plays in ordinary video players.

Source recordings are not modified. Use a new output folder after changing settings or the included videos. Cancellation leaves an incomplete working folder with diagnostics; it does not publish a finished result.

Learn with two practice recordings

Choose Help → Try practice recordings. The app copies the bundled synthetic videos and data to a fresh practice folder and scans them. No real driver footage or recorded locations are included.

  1. Leave both videos ticked and check their upright previews.
  2. Untick the first run. Notice that the included count and overlap change.
  3. Choose Driving data, with Scene blank, then Frame & preview….
  4. Create files and open the MP4. Only your included run is exported.

Only overlapping VBOX samples are linked. By default, output video contains only matching periods, including the frames containing the boundary samples. Separate periods use separate clips. VBOX video times are adjusted automatically. Untick Only export video with VBOX data to retain whole video chapters; the overlay disappears outside data coverage. This demo tests matching and video output; it cannot prove recorder authenticity.

Choose the picture you want

Drag to frame your video. Keep the original VBOX videos beside the VBO files, select a GoPro row, then open Frame & preview…. Tick Crop to VBOX and drag the outlined box on the left. The right pane shows the finished picture with your selected overlay. The crop keeps the original VBOX proportions, normally 16:9. Arrow keys make small adjustments; Shift + arrows moves further. Centre crop resets the position. Click Apply to use it or Cancel to keep the previous framing. Untick cropping to retain the whole image.

Rotation is applied before cropping, and overlays are fitted afterwards. Cropping changes the picture, not the timing or data. The separate Only export video with VBOX data checkbox trims time: sound, overlays and VBOX links are adjusted together, and full-session data remains available for lap history. Each video has its own choice. Cropping needs all original VBOX video chapters linked to the matched data to have the same shape; see Details if it is unavailable.

OverlayWhat appears
NoneCorrected video without data burned into the picture.
Driving dataSpeed, RPM, throttle and brake. Leave Scene blank for the built-in dashboard (mph, rpm, %, psi), or choose a supported scene. Your VBO must contain the required channels and compatible units.
Full scene · no rear cameraSupported gauges, artwork, G-ball, track map and lap/delta timing from your selected scene. Rear-camera picture and its border are excluded. Uniform scaling preserves gauge and map proportions.

Reading a .VVHSN scene requires VBOX Video Setup installed separately. This app uses its locally installed library; it does not include vendor software or scene artwork. Scene support covers a tested subset; unsupported widgets or units stop export with an explanation. Derived lap/delta and G-ball values can differ from the recorder.

Full resolution keeps the pixels remaining after rotation and cropping. All modes encode a new H.264 video with compression loss. HD and Compact limit the longest edge to 1920 and 1280 pixels. No option copies the original compressed stream.

If something needs attention

What you seeWhat to do
No matchesOpen Details. Check that the GoPro MP4 files are directly in the folder with your VBOX runs, from the same vehicle and time. Filenames do not supply recording times. GPS-less GoPros, including HERO12, cannot be matched automatically.
Choose rotationSelect the video and inspect the preview, then choose the correct rotation. The app defaults to no rotation; visually check every run and change it if necessary.
Missing channels / unsupported sceneCheck the scene, VBO channel names and units. Try Driving data with Scene blank, or None if the data lacks throttle, RPM or brake.
Output folder already existsAn identical verified rerun may be reused. Otherwise choose a new name in Save to. Existing results are protected.
Video tools unavailableUse Help → Set up video tools. Check the connection and free disk space. For offline use, an existing FFmpeg/FFprobe installation on PATH or in Circuit Tools 3 is supported.
Circuit Tools checksum errorThis is a known unresolved limitation. The MP4 can still be viewed separately. Keep the originals. Do not interpret successful export checks as a checksum fix.
Unexpected failureRead Details and the output Report. Describe your camera model, app version and error in a GitHub issue. Review logs before posting: they may contain filenames, local paths and GPS data. Share only material you intend to make public.
