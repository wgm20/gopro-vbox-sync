From download to your first run

  1. Install and open the app. Video tools download on request (110 MB).
  2. Choose Help → Try practice recordings to try the included demo.
  3. Browse to your GoPro/VBOX folder, scan and tick the videos to include.
  4. Check the preview, choose your overlay and create the files.

The illustrated quick-start guide works offline. All processing stays on your computer.
