GoPro VBOX Sync is free and open source.

GETTING STARTED
No Python installation is required. The app offers a verified download of FFmpeg video tools when needed (about 110 MB). Practice recordings and an offline guide are included. Full-scene overlays require a supported scene and a separate local installation of VBOX Video Setup.

Independent project, not affiliated with GoPro or Racelogic. Third-party notices are included in Help.
